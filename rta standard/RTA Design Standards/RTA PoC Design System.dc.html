03 · Brand foundations

Typography — quiet luxury, loud scale

Primary typeface: the official RTA family (Light / Regular / Bold / Black; Latin + Arabic). Stack: "RTA", "Segoe UI", "Noto Sans Arabic", sans-serif. This specimen uses Hanken Grotesk as a stand-in until the licensed RTA files are installed. Classiness comes from scale contrast and weight discipline: big light numbers, quiet body, tracked caps labels.
